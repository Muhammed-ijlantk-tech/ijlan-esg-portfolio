BRSR & GRI Disclosure Analysis

GRI to BRSR mapping of Infosys environmental disclosures

Project type: Disclosure analysisTools: ExcelFrameworks: GRI · BRSR (references)Data: Infosys ESG Data Book 2024–25, GRI content index

01 — Objective

Map the environmental GRI disclosures in the Infosys GRI content index to the BRSR references given in the same index, and compare them with the ESG data extracted in Projects 1 and 2.

05 — Key Findings

Most environmental GRI disclosures link to BRSR Principle 6

16 of the 21 disclosures point to a Principle 6 indicator: 13 Essential, 1 Leadership and 2 both.

Scope 1 and 2 are Essential, Scope 3 is Leadership

Scope 1 and 2 (GRI 305-1 and 305-2) map to Essential indicator E7. Scope 3 (GRI 305-3) maps to Leadership indicator L2, which is the more advanced level of BRSR reporting.

The five without a BRSR link are mostly descriptive

Four of them (303-1, 303-2, 306-1 and 306-2) describe water and waste management and are reported in the ESG Report. 305-6 (ozone-depleting substances) is reported in the Data Book only.

My work covers about half of the disclosures

10 of 21 are covered fully or partly. The largest gap is water (303-3 to 303-5), and waste is only partly covered because Project 1 holds hazardous waste records only.

08 — Limitations & Disclaimer

Limitations: the BRSR references come from the Data Book's GRI content index. The BRSR itself, which is in the Infosys Integrated Annual Report, was not read, so no BRSR values were extracted and BRSR Core is not covered. Only the 21 environmental disclosures are included, and the reporting period is April 1, 2024 to March 31, 2025.

This is an independent analysis of public Infosys information. It is not an official GRI or BRSR assessment, assurance conclusion or certification, and the Infosys disclosures remain the reference.
