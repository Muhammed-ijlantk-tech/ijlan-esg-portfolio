About

Turning environmental data into measurable ESG insights.

I am an MSc Environmental Science graduate focused on ESG data analytics, carbon accounting and sustainability reporting. My academic and independent projects combine environmental research with corporate ESG analysis, data modelling and visualisation.

I work with sustainability disclosures and environmental datasets to structure, analyse and communicate ESG information using Excel, Power BI and QGIS, with working knowledge of BRSR/BRSR Core, GRI, ISSB/IFRS S1 & S2 and the GHG Protocol.
